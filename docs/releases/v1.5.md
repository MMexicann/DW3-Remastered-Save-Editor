# Universal Koei Tecmo Save Editor v1.5

Four additional Windows PC editors and expanded inventory controls, with
Mexican's branding, Light/Dark themes, backups, Undo and Review Changes.

- **Dynasty Warriors 7 XL Definitive:** gold, officer stats and skill points,
  switching between owned equipped weapons, and weapon/skill inspection.
- **Warriors Orochi 3 Ultimate Definitive:** officer stats, growth points, gems,
  attribute orbs, crafting materials and existing weapon attributes.
- **Samurai Warriors 4 DX:** gold, gems, officer stats and playable unlocks,
  equipped weapons and attached weapon skills.
- **Pirate Warriors 4:** Beli and quantities of owned coins, with searchable
  coin records and resource history.
- **Dynasty Warriors 8 XL:** weapon-action compatibility, weapon affinity and
  ally inspection.
- **Atelier Sophie 2:** consumable refills and searchable inventory/equipment.

**Find records** searches every inspector column. Bulk actions follow your
visible field selection, while higher existing values are preserved.

Download **UniversalKoeiTecmoSaveEditor-v1.5.exe** and run it, or use the
**Windows ZIP** for the executable and documentation. No Python installation is
needed. Open a separate save copy, review your edits and use **Save As**.
