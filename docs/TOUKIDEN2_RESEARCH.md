# Toukiden 2 Windows investigation

Investigated 2026-10-11. **No Toukiden 2 gameplay adapter is registered.** A
publicly shared Windows save folder was acquired and inspected, but its native
codec, integrity and revision were not proved. This is a specific format blocker,
not a claim that a working editor cannot be written.

## Acquired file observations

The archive has twelve gameplay files:
`play00_`, `play01_` and `play02_`, each with `user.bin`, `chkp.bin`,
`safe.bin` and `btle.bin` suffixes. Each is **497,931 bytes (`0x7990B`)**.
`system.bin` is **39,565 bytes (`0x9A8D`)**. These are observations of one
folder, not universal accepted lengths or proven save-role semantics.

Four apparently uninitialized `safe`/`btle` files are byte-identical and have
low byte entropy. Their first twelve bytes are
`00 23 01 17 03 00 00 00 0A 00 00 00`. The byte-identical uninitialized
`user`/`chkp` variants and populated files have near-eight-bit entropy. The
first twelve encrypted bytes of an uninitialized variant are
`87 28 3F 0F 2A CC 3B 55 B5 82 9B 95`. These prefixes are observations;
their title/revision/control interpretation is not established.

Assuming the plain and encrypted uninitialized variants encode the same state
produces an XOR mask and a plausible populated candidate. This remains a
**private hypothesis**: the pairs were not generated by a controlled unchanged
game save. Populated peers differ beyond the header by hundreds of bytes; the
manual, checkpoint, safe and battle files cannot be substituted for one another.
The candidate is not accepted by a public parser, does not expose writable
fields and is not evidence of edited game-load validation.

Private analytical checks rejected exact XOR-mask periodicity for powers of
two from 1 through 4,096 and for 65,536; the existing Koei byte LCG did not
recover a matching seed at several candidate starts, and the existing Koei
word recurrence did not predict adjacent candidate masks. Other common LCG
and MT19937 hypotheses also failed. Byte sums and CRC32 did not establish the
apparent header integrity value. These are rejected hypotheses, not native
codec tests. No damaged file was repaired and no integrity check was bypassed.

## Mechanics and precise implementation blockers

The official manual, especially pages 13-16, 21, 48-58, establishes the
following distinctions. Every row is blocked from writes until the native
format, integrity and controlled Windows mapping qualify.

| Mechanic | Established gameplay distinction | Specific remaining mapping evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haku | Spendable currency acquired through purification, quests/missions and deposits; used for equipment, shopping and facilities. | A displayed balance before/after one purchase or fixed reward, unchanged control, stored width and natural cap. Separate balance from reward history. |
| Existing materials | Materials support equipment creation/upgrade; later synthesis and machina development are separate operations. First ownership and quantities may affect availability. | Existing ordinary-material count pair, ID/count/record-order/empty-slot proof, quest-item distinction and first-acquisition/list-notification dependencies. Console stock-filling codes do not qualify PC records. |
| Weapons and armor | Owned equipment, equipped references and effects differ. Weapon attack and armor defense are equipment-derived. | One acquired/equipped item pair with displayed ID/name and full record; capacity/sentinel rules and reference integrity. No insertion or invented equipment IDs. |
| Fortification / reinforcement | Blacksmith fortification upgrades an existing weapon or armor. | One fortification step pair, cost and materials consumed, level/EXP/stat relationship and actual limits; no assumed Kiwami upgrade rules. |
| Reforging | Blacksmith reforging makes equipment from existing equipment and is distinct from fortification. | One reforge pair proving input consumption, resulting identity, inherited state and recipe/story prerequisites. |
| Mitama ownership and equipment | Defeating Oni frees Mitama; weapon, armor and Demon Hand assignments provide different abilities. | One acquisition pair and separate equip pair, supported identities, slots, first-acquisition/notification state and ownership validation. |
| Boost development and selection | Equipped Mitama develop through actions; up to three Boosts can be selected. Shrine prayers accelerate development or improve discovery. Combination Boosts depend on relationships among Mitama. | A controlled development pair and boost-selection pair; unlock/level/EXP/dependency rules and relevant equipped references. Planner skill names are not serialized IDs. |
| Skills | Weapon Mitama provide battle-style Skills; armor provides Evasion Skills and Demon Hand provides Attack Skills. Some abilities require story progress. Stocks/gauges during battle differ from persistent ownership/development. | Persistent versus transient storage, story prerequisite and active-skill stock/gauge mapping. Runtime trainer addresses are excluded. |
| Persistent progression | Story unlocks facilities, shop stock, abilities, synthesis, machina and other options; mission/quest completion produces rewards. | One quest/mission/story action pair with preconditions, completion, availability, claimed rewards and notification state; separate explicit progression actions from resource edits. |

## Validation and next inputs

Acquisition: one freely shared Windows folder, thirteen `.bin` files inspected
as data. **Qualified genuine unchanged roundtrips: 0. Procedural editor tests:
0. Surgical edit/integrity tests: 0. Backup/Undo/Review/GUI editor tests: 0.
Actual edited game-load/re-save validation: 0.** There is no editor to claim
coverage for those workflows; existing-game regressions are recorded by the
overall assignment separately.

The immediate input needed is an owner-reviewed native Windows build and its
save read/write/integrity routine available for static analysis, or a
source-backed codec with matching controlled encrypted/plaintext references.
Also needed are an unchanged control and one-action pairs with build, language,
DLC, actual slot/role, displayed before/after values and acquisition provenance.
Retain every original partner and `system.bin` privately to qualify cross-file
relations. A Windows game owner must finally load and re-save each edited case;
an arithmetic roundtrip alone cannot establish that result.
