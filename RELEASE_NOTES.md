# Dynasty Warriors 3 Remastered Save Editor v1.0

v1.0 adds music gallery unlocks and bulk element editing for unique weapons, with a refreshed interface and a dedicated Collections tab.

- Unlock all 42 music tracks and 50 movies.
- Choose Fire, Lightning, Steel or Wind for an individual weapon or all owned unique weapons, including weapons queued for acquisition.
- Change a supported weapon's element while preserving unusual saved bonuses and all its other flags.
- Use supported Max values while keeping higher existing item and weapon values.
- Contact Mexican through the new Discord and Steam links in the program.

The editor also supports all 42 officers, 16 normal and 27 rare items, 84 unique weapons including Ziluan's 4th/5th weapons, normal and rare weapon bonuses, the weapon collection, Tactics costumes, bodyguard growth and equipment, appearances and colors, 108 playable stages, three side stories, 39 Musou story clears, campaign save removal and Huanglong Elixirs from 0 to 999.

Download and extract the Windows ZIP, then run `DW3RemasteredSaveEditor-v1.0.exe`. No Python or installer is needed. Open a separate copy of `GameStatusData.sav`, keep an untouched backup, review your changes and use **Save As** to create an edited copy. Automatic backups, Undo and backup restore are included.
